Conventions used for the 36 points

Almanacs and apps agree on most of the 36 points but differ on a few table cells. So that you can check a result, these are the conventions in src/guna.js. The groom's values come first in each table lookup unless noted.

KootaMaxRead fromRule used here
Varna1Moon signCancer, Scorpio, Pisces Brahmin; Aries, Leo, Sagittarius Kshatriya; Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn Vaishya; Gemini, Libra, Aquarius Shudra. 1 point when the groom's varna is equal to or higher than the bride's.
Vashya2Moon signWhole-sign groups (Chatushpada, Manava, Jalachara, Vanachara, Keeta) with the graded 2 / 1 / ½ / 0 table most calculators print. Sagittarius is Manava and Capricorn is Jalachara; no split by degree.
Tara3NakshatraCounted inclusively from each star to the other. A remainder of 3, 5 or 7 (after dividing by 9) is unfavourable. 1.5 points for each favourable direction.
Yoni4NakshatraSymmetric 14-animal table, 0 for the seven enemy pairs.
Graha Maitri5Lords of the Moon signsNatural friendship of the two sign lords, taken both ways: 5, 4, 3, 1, ½ or 0.
Gana6Nakshatra6 for the same gana. Deva groom with Manushya bride 5, the reverse 6. Rakshasa bride with Deva groom 1. Other Rakshasa pairs 0.
Bhakoot7Distance between Moon signs0 when the signs are 2/12, 5/9 or 6/8 apart, otherwise 7. Traditional cancellations (same sign lord, or friendly lords with a clean nadi) are reported in notes; the points are not changed.
Nadi8Nakshatra0 when both stars are in the same nadi (Adi, Madhya or Antya), otherwise 8.

Checks

The test suite includes a koota-by-koota worked example (groom Rohini in Vrishabha, bride Dhanishta in Kumbha: 27 of 36) and checks that every one of the possible nakshatra and rashi pairs gives a total between 0 and 36 that equals the sum of its eight parts. Some almanacs split Sagittarius and Capricorn by degree, use a different gana or yoni cell, or fold bhakoot cancellations into the points, so another app can show a point or two more or less. If you can point to a published table that disagrees with one of these cells, please open an issue.

Nakshatra lookup

The sidereal zodiac is divided into 27 nakshatras of 13°20′ each, each with four padas of 3°20′, and 12 rashis of 30°. A sidereal Moon longitude therefore gives the nakshatra, pada and rashi directly. The library does not calculate the Moon's position from a birth time; that needs an ephemeris and a choice of ayanamsa.

Limits

A guna score compares the two Moons only. It does not look at Manglik dosh, the 7th house, dasha periods or the rest of the two charts, and it should never override the couple's own consent.
